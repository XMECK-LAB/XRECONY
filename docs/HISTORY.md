# XRECONY history

Public versioning is intentionally simple.

## Public releases

- **XRECONY 1.0 — Engineering Preview**  
  First public engineering release, distilled from the mature pre-2.0 working lineage.

- **XRECONY 2.0 — Public Preview**  
  Current product line with a more complete evidence-backed workflow, larger observed workloads, drift reporting, export/report surfaces, and copy-first Safe Realization.

## Internal engineering lineage

Before the public 2.0 line, XRECONY evolved through multiple internal Surface, XRF, RSF and V4–V11 engineering stages.

Those names remain valuable engineering provenance, but they are intentionally **not** exposed as public product-version numbering.

Public users should only need to understand:

```text
XRECONY 1.0
Engineering Preview
      ↓
XRECONY 2.0
Public Preview
```

## Identity evolution

XRECONY originated in the broader reconstruction/survivability research direction associated with [XPADI-SGDS](https://github.com/raajmandale/XPADI-SGDS).

The public working-software repository is now published under [XMECK-LAB](https://github.com/XMECK-LAB), while creator/research provenance remains attached to [Raaj Mandale](https://github.com/raajmandale).

This creates a deliberate separation:

**research lineage → engineering lineage → working public software**
