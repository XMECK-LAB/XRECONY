# XRECONY history

Public versioning is intentionally simple.

- **1.0 — Engineering Preview**: distilled from the mature pre-2.0 engineering lineage.
- **2.0 — Public Preview**: current product line.

Internally the project evolved through multiple Surface/XRF/RSF and V4–V11 engineering stages before the 2.0 reconstruction program. Those names remain internal history and are not public product versions.
