# XRECONY public engineering roadmap

XRECONY 2.0 is usable engineering software, but the original reconstruction vision is not considered complete.

## Scaling
- controlled 100 GB–1 TB+ workload matrix
- object-count/topology-aware benchmark reporting
- HDD/SSD/NAS comparisons
- memory, cancellation and resume profiling

## Duplicate intelligence
- stronger content-level confirmation
- duplicate-confidence evidence
- better version-family separation
- safer resolution planning

## Reconstruction intelligence
- deeper relationship explanations
- improved drift diagnostics
- stronger generation comparison

## Providers
- provider-specific authentication
- pagination/checkpoints
- consent/revocation handling
- qualification before enablement

## Productization
- signed Windows artifacts
- clean-machine lifecycle qualification
- reproducible release manifests
- update strategy

## Long-term research objective

25 TB-class reconstruction around ~45 minutes remains an engineering target, not a current capability claim.
